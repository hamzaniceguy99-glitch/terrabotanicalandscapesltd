How we work

A landscaper that works around the nesting season

Terra Botanica Landscapes Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 15967280, based at Bittering Street in Gressenhall near Dereham, Norfolk, and trading since September 2024. Being that new, the company claims no track record it does not have. The work is garden design and planting, hedges and trees, hard landscaping and grounds maintenance.

The constraint that shapes the calendar is legal rather than horticultural. Under the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 it is an offence to damage or destroy a wild bird's nest while it is in use or being built, and the offence is committed by whoever does the cutting — a client's instruction is not a defence. So hedges, shrubs and ivy are inspected before work in the nesting season, and sometimes the job waits. Separately, a tree under a Tree Preservation Order, or a qualifying tree in a conservation area, needs the council's consent before any work, with an unlimited fine for getting it wrong. And green waste taken off a site has to travel under waste carrier registration to an authorised site, with the transfer documented.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Why can you not just cut the hedge now?

Because if something is nesting in it, cutting it is a criminal offence under the Wildlife and Countryside Act, and the offence belongs to the person holding the tool. We inspect before cutting, particularly between March and August. Most of the time it is fine and we proceed. Occasionally it is not, and then the job waits until the birds have fledged.

I am the homeowner and I am telling you to do it. Does that not cover you?

No, and it would not cover you either. An instruction from a client is not a defence to a wildlife offence. This is one of the few things where we will decline a direct instruction, and we would rather explain why now than have the conversation afterwards.

How do I know if my tree is protected?

We check with the local planning authority before working on it. A Tree Preservation Order can apply to a single tree, a group or a whole woodland, and most trees of qualifying size in a conservation area are protected too. Pruning or felling one without consent can bring an unlimited fine, so it is not something to take a neighbour's word on.

Can you just take the green waste away in your trailer?

Yes, because we are registered to carry it and it goes to an authorised site with the transfer documented. The reason to care is that fly-tipped waste gets traced back to the property it came from, which is how homeowners end up answering for a contractor who dumped it in a lane.

I think we have Japanese knotweed. Can you dig it out?

Not as part of ordinary landscaping. Soil containing knotweed rhizome is controlled waste requiring a permitted facility, causing it to grow in the wild is an offence, and its presence can create civil liability towards neighbours. We will report it to you in writing and point you to a specialist with a management plan rather than spreading it around your garden.

Will the planting survive?

We prepare properly and we plant well, and we will not guarantee establishment. Survival depends mostly on watering and aftercare in the first two seasons, plus soil and weather. We write the aftercare down because it matters more than anything we do on planting day.
