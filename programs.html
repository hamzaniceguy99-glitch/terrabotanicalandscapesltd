Four services, with the wildlife and tree checks done before any cutting.

Program 01 · Checked, then cut

Hedges and trees

Two checks happen before the tools come out: is anything nesting, and is the tree protected. Both carry criminal liability for the person doing the work, which is why we do not take a client's assurance on either. If a hedge has an active nest in it, we come back. If a tree is under an order, we wait for consent.

Program 02 · Soil decides it

Garden design and planting

Most planting failures are a soil and water problem rather than a plant problem, so we test what you have rather than working from a plan drawn elsewhere. We will also say when a client's chosen plant will not thrive in that position, and we do not guarantee establishment — survival depends on aftercare and weather more than on the day of planting.

Program 03 · Drainage first

Hard landscaping

A patio laid without thinking about where the water goes becomes somebody's damp problem, and run-off onto a neighbour's land becomes an argument. We resolve drainage before surfacing and we mention permeability, because paving over a front garden can require planning permission depending on area and drainage.

Program 04 · Waste leaves properly

Grounds maintenance

Green waste taken off your site is our waste to handle lawfully: registered carriage, an authorised site, and the paperwork that proves it. Fly-tipped green waste is traced back to the property it came from, which is how a homeowner ends up answering for a contractor's shortcut. We also report suspected knotweed in writing rather than quietly digging it.
